The Tetris of Logistics: 3D Bin Packing

My article about the ICT field of work. I picked 3D bin packing because I worked on this problem in my graduation project (maturita), so I was curious how it is used in real logistics software.

Short summary of the article:

  • 3D bin packing means arranging items of different sizes into containers such as boxes, pallets or trucks, so that as little space and money as possible is wasted.
  • Software solves it with heuristics (fast rules of thumb, not always optimal), exact algorithms (always optimal, but too slow for big inputs) or metaheuristics (a compromise that keeps improving a first solution).
  • The best result is not always the fullest box. Carrier prices depend on weight and size limits, so splitting one order into two cartons can be much cheaper. The article gives an example with 42% lower transport costs.
