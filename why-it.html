Why ICT

I chose ICT because I enjoy solving problems and finding new ways to make things work. At first, the career opportunities, good salary and flexible working hours (and, of course, having enough money for alcohol) were what pulled me in. Somewhere along the way I realised I actually enjoy programming, mostly because of the feeling of finally solving a problem after struggling with it for way too long.

During my HBO-ICT studies I want to get better at working in a team and actually being useful in one. Working in a group also means there is always someone to blame when the code breaks, which is obviously very important. I also want to understand bigger systems and data structures instead of only staring at small pieces of code, and gain experience through coding opportunities at school or with companies, with the goal of eventually becoming a backend developer.
