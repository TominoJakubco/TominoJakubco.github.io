My Future in IT

The IT direction that interests me most is software engineering, especially backend development. Backend developers build and maintain the parts of applications that users do not see, such as APIs, databases and server-side logic. This work requires problem-solving, logical thinking, programming skills and an understanding of databases, security and software architecture.

In four years, I hope to be working as a software engineer or backend developer at a company that builds useful software and gives developers opportunities to learn. During my studies, I want to improve my skills in programming, databases, APIs and designing reliable applications. I also want to gain practical experience through team projects and work with real clients. My goal is to become a developer who can understand a problem, design a sensible solution and build software that is reliable, maintainable and useful.
