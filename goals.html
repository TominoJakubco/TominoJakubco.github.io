My SWOT Analysis

Strengths Weaknesses
  • Skilled: I pick up new languages and technologies quickly, and I'm comfortable in Java and C#.
  • Analytical: I split big problems into small steps and solve them one by one.
  • Determined:If I get really interested in a problem or topic I can stay up all night working on it without getting tired or bored.
  • Procrastination: I tend to start assignments way too close to the deadline.
  • Presenting: Speaking in front of a group stresses me out.
  • Unseriousness: I often joke around and don't take things seriously enough, which can make me look unprofessional.
Opportunities Threats
  • Internships at software companies in the region.
  • Teachers and fellow students who can help me with planning.
  • New technologies and programming languages to learn, which can help me become a better developer.
  • Laziness: I often leave things up until the very last minute which causes me to stress and panic which in turn makes me even more scared to actaully start working on said thing.
  • Hobbies: League of Legends has a way of eating entire evenings.

My SMART Study Goals

Goal Specific & Measurable Deadline
Goal 1
(Strength: analytical thinking)		 Practise problem solving by solving algorithm exercises on a website like Codewars or LeetCode. I solve at least 2 exercises every week until I have solved 20 in total, and for each one I write down how I split it into steps. 20 exercises solved by 8 January 2027.

My Mission This Year

As an HBO-ICT student, I am working on becoming a backend developer, one solved problem at a time. I am sharpening my analytical thinking by solving algorithm exercises every week, so I can handle bigger problems with confidence. This year I am also learning to present my work without panicking, by practising it out loud before every presentation. My goal is to finish this year as a student who can both build good software and explain it to others.
